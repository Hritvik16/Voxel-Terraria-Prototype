
================================================================================================
DENSE vs TILED FLUID ADDRESSING -- WALL-CLOCK A/B   [PROVISIONAL]
================================================================================================
RELEASE standalone, launched outside the Editor. vsync off, uncapped.
Wall clock from Time.unscaledDeltaTime. ONE CONFIG PER PROCESS LAUNCH.
180 samples after 420 warmup + 45 settle frames, discarded warm-up launch first.

NO GPU TIMING ANYWHERE. gpuFrameTime is read nowhere (Amendment 8.10:
inflated ~2.6-2.7x on this hardware). No Xcode, no Instruments (8.9 Rule 1).
There is no Performance State field -- FrameTimingManager cannot report it
on this platform; that is a permanent accepted limitation, not worked around.

READING RULE. 'frame' is the WHOLE frame: CA submit, the GPU catching up,
the op-list readback, the CPU applying it, and the clipmap uploads that
follow. It is not a fluid-kernel figure and may not be quoted as one.
§2.2's <=3.5 ms fluid budget is a GPU-LANE budget; a wall-clock frame
total is a different quantity, so no row below is scored pass/fail against it.
